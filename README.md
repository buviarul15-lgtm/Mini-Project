## 📊 Project Overview
The analysis focuses on various dimensions such as product types, payment methods, customer demographics, time trends monthly , and geographical locations. Interactive features like slicers enable users to explore the data dynamically and make comparisons across different categories.By using this dashboard, stakeholders can identify top-performing products, understand customer preferences, evaluate sales trends, and detect patterns that impact business performance. The insights generated help in making informed decisions related to marketing strategies, inventory management, and overall business growth.

## 🗂️ Data Source
Google Dataset Search - 2020-2025.
- [SIZE](#-SIZE)
The dataset contains 1000 rows and 11 columns, representing transaction-level sales data of the Pure Bake Cookies business.
- [KEY VARIABLES](#-KEY-VARIABLES)
Transaction Date, Biscuit Brand, Quantity Purchased, Payment Mode, Buyer Location, Sales Representative, Discount, and customer demographic details like Age Group and Gender.

  ## 🔍 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- What are the overall sales trends over time (daily, weekly, monthly)?
- Which biscuit brands generate the highest sales?
- How do discounts impact quantity purchased and revenue?
- What is the distribution of customers by age group and gender?
- Which locations contribute the most to total sales?
- What are the most preferred payment modes?
- How do sales vary between weekdays and weekends?
- Who are the top-performing sales representatives?

  ## 💡 Key Insights
- Shortbread and Vanilla cookies are top-performing products
- Weekdays generate higher profit compared to weekends
- Digital payments (Mobile, Debit Card) are widely preferred
- Certain cities contribute more significantly to sales
- Sales show seasonal variation across months.

  ## 🚀 Recommendations
- Use discounts strategically to boost sales volume while maintaining profit margins.
- Increase production and promotion of top-selling biscuit brands to maximize revenue.
- Design marketing campaigns focused on dominant age groups and frequent buyers.
- Invest more in regions with strong sales and improve strategies in low-performing areas.
- Introduce special weekend offers and promotions to capitalize on higher demand.
- Promote digital payment methods for faster and more convenient transactions.
- Provide training and incentives to boost underperforming sales representatives.
- Continuously monitor trends and use data insights to plan inventory and sales strategies.
